# ONEDRAFT™

## GLOBAL MARKET ECONOMY AND CORRELATED SECTOR OPPORTUNITY

### GLOBAL ECONOMIC POSITION

OneDraft™ operates at the intersection of several of the world's largest economic and technology sectors. While its immediate commercial market is the software and digital-services layer supporting architecture, engineering, construction, and the built environment, the economic activity influenced by those systems extends into multi-trillion-dollar global sectors.

OneDraft™ should therefore be understood not solely as a CAD product, but as an AI-native project intelligence and technical documentation platform positioned within the broader digital infrastructure of the built environment.

### PRIMARY ECONOMIC ENVIRONMENT

The global construction economy represents approximately **$15–20 trillion in annual economic activity**, depending upon geographic and industry definitions.

Construction represents the ultimate economic environment in which architectural documentation, engineering design, building information, technical coordination, project documentation, construction execution, and asset information are converted into physical infrastructure.

OneDraft™ does not claim the entire construction economy as software revenue. Rather, this sector represents the economic environment whose productivity, coordination, information quality, and documentation processes the platform is designed to influence.

### ENGINEERING SERVICES

The global engineering-services economy represents approximately **$2–4 trillion annually**, depending upon market definition and inclusion of engineering disciplines and related professional services.

This represents a major potential economic environment for OneDraft™, particularly as the platform expands beyond architectural drafting into structural engineering, building systems, civil infrastructure, technical analysis, engineering documentation, coordination, and automated design intelligence.

### ARCHITECTURE, ENGINEERING, AND CONSTRUCTION SERVICES

The global architectural, engineering, and construction professional-services economy represents approximately **$100–500 billion annually**, depending upon the boundaries used to define the sector.

This is one of OneDraft™'s most directly relevant service markets because the platform is designed around the transformation of requirements and design intent into coordinated technical documentation.

### CAD SOFTWARE

The global CAD software market is approximately **$6–10 billion annually**, depending upon market definition.

CAD represents one of OneDraft™'s most immediate software categories.

However, OneDraft™ is not intended to remain limited to conventional computer-aided drafting. Its architecture introduces a canonical project model, structured building information, natural-language commands, design proposals, relationships, constraints, revisions, and intelligent documentation.

Accordingly, OneDraft™ is positioned to expand the value proposition beyond conventional geometry-based CAD.

### CAD AND PLM SOFTWARE

The broader global CAD and product-lifecycle-management software economy is approximately **$15–20 billion annually**.

This broader category is relevant because OneDraft™ incorporates concepts analogous to structured model information, versioning, revisions, change history, relationships, and lifecycle information.

OneDraft™ applies those principles specifically to the built environment and architectural, engineering, and construction workflows.

### BUILDING INFORMATION MODELING

The global BIM software market is approximately **$9–10 billion annually**, with substantial projected growth over the coming decade.

BIM represents a particularly important adjacent market because OneDraft™ is based upon structured project information rather than treating drawings as isolated graphical artifacts.

The OneDraft™ canonical domain model provides a foundation for representing buildings, levels, spaces, elements, assemblies, parameters, constraints, relationships, geometry references, documentation, revisions, and project history as interconnected information.

### CONSTRUCTION TECHNOLOGY

The global construction-technology ecosystem represents **tens of billions of dollars in annual technology expenditure**, depending upon the definition used.

### DIGITAL TWINS AND DIGITAL ENGINEERING

Digital-twin, digital-engineering, and connected-model technologies represent **tens of billions of dollars in global economic activity and software expenditure**, with substantial growth expected as infrastructure and buildings become increasingly data-driven.

OneDraft™'s persistent canonical project model provides a potential foundation for connecting design information with later project stages, including construction, commissioning, as-built documentation, maintenance, and asset information.

### ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE

Artificial intelligence represents a rapidly expanding global technology economy measured in the **hundreds of billions of dollars**, with applications extending across virtually every major industry.

OneDraft™ occupies a specialized vertical position within this economy by applying AI to architectural, engineering, construction, building-information, technical-documentation, and project-model workflows.

### THE INFORMATION ECONOMY OF THE BUILT ENVIRONMENT

The central commercial thesis of OneDraft™ is that the value of architectural and engineering software is not limited to the lines, surfaces, models, or drawings it produces.

The greater opportunity is the information connecting those artifacts.

A building begins as requirements and intent.

Those requirements become spaces, systems, elements, assemblies, constraints, relationships, and geometry.

Those structured objects become drawings, schedules, annotations, specifications, revisions, redlines, and construction documentation.

Those documents become construction activity.

Construction produces revisions, as-built information, commissioning information, operational records, and ultimately asset information.

OneDraft™ is designed to maintain continuity across this information chain.

### LONG-TERM OPPORTUNITY

The long-term OneDraft™ opportunity therefore extends beyond CAD replacement.

The platform can develop toward an integrated information layer spanning:

**Requirements → Design → Engineering → BIM → Documentation → Code Analysis → Coordination → Construction → Revision → As-Built → Operations → Asset Information**

This creates a potential transition from software that merely produces drawings toward software that maintains a persistent computational representation of the project itself.
